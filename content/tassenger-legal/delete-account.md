## Quick summary

- You can request deletion inside Tassenger or from this web page.
- Uninstalling Tassenger does not delete your account.
- We verify requests before deleting accounts to prevent someone else deleting your account.
- Active deletion/anonymization usually completes within 30 days after verification.
- Backup copies may take up to 90 days to age out.
- Some shared, official workspace, legal, security, abuse-prevention, and audit records may be retained or anonymized instead of hard-deleted.

## How to request deletion

### From inside Tassenger

1. Open Tassenger.
2. Go to the **Me** tab.
3. Choose **Delete account**.
4. Read the deletion summary.
5. Submit the deletion request.

The app may ask you to confirm your phone number or re-verify ownership before the request is processed.

### From this web page

Submit your deletion request from [https://www.virtualifyme.com/tassenger/delete-account](https://www.virtualifyme.com/tassenger/delete-account).

If the website form is unavailable, email [vscsocialhandle@gmail.com](mailto:vscsocialhandle@gmail.com) with the subject **Tassenger account deletion request** and include:

- phone number on the account, including country code;
- display name on the account;
- optional email on your profile, if any;
- reason for deletion, if you want to share it;
- a contact email or phone number where we can verify the request.

## Identity verification

We verify deletion requests to protect your account. Verification may include:

- OTP sent to the phone number on the account;
- confirmation from the optional email on your profile;
- additional account details needed to identify the account;
- workspace-admin coordination where the request affects organization-owned workspace records.

If we cannot verify that you control the account, we may decline the request or ask for more information.

## What we delete or anonymize

After verification, the deletion/anonymization workflow covers:

### Account and sign-in data

- phone number removed from the active account where possible;
- active sessions invalidated;
- OTP/session data expired or removed according to security retention;
- device push tokens removed so notifications stop for the deleted account.

### Profile data

- name removed or replaced with a generic deleted-user label;
- About line removed;
- optional email removed;
- profile photo removed from active storage.

### Contacts and discovery

- contact discovery links tied to your account are removed or anonymized;
- your account no longer appears as an active Tassenger profile through contact matching.

### Messages and personal groups

- messages you sent may be deleted, anonymized, or replaced with a deleted-message tombstone such as **This message was deleted**;
- group membership records may be removed or anonymized;
- other users may still see message context, tombstones, quoted history, screenshots, exports, or media they already saved outside Tassenger.

### Tasks and task history

- personal task data tied only to your account is removed or anonymized where possible;
- shared task records may keep history with your identity replaced by an anonymized label so the task record remains understandable to other participants.

### Media

- profile photos, group photos owned by your account, chat images, proof images, and voice notes tied to your account are removed from active storage where possible;
- copies already downloaded, screenshotted, forwarded, exported, or stored outside Tassenger cannot be removed by us;
- backup copies may remain until the backup cycle expires.

### Notifications and support records

- device push tokens are removed;
- support access grants are revoked;
- support and audit records may be retained in minimized or anonymized form for safety, abuse prevention, security, and legal reasons.

## Official Workspaces

If you used Tassenger inside an Official Workspace:

- your personal account can still be deleted or anonymized;
- organization-owned workspace records may remain under the workspace owner's control;
- task history, reports, audit logs, role changes, and accountability records may be retained so the organization has a coherent record;
- your identity is anonymized where possible, unless retention in identifiable form is required by law, security, dispute handling, or workspace integrity.

If you are the only owner/admin of a workspace, we may ask you to transfer ownership or coordinate with the organization before completing parts of the deletion workflow that affect workspace administration.

## What may be retained

We may retain limited data where necessary for:

- legal compliance;
- security and abuse prevention;
- fraud, spam, or account-takeover investigation;
- dispute handling;
- support audit logs;
- Official Workspace accountability;
- backup integrity until backups expire.

Retained records are limited to what is necessary for those purposes and are protected under our security controls.

## Timing

Typical timing after we receive a request:

- verification: within 7 business days;
- active-system deletion/anonymization: within 30 days after verification;
- backup aging: up to 90 days after active deletion.

Some requests may take longer if verification is incomplete, the account is tied to an active legal/security issue, or an Official Workspace ownership transfer is needed.

## Deletion does not cancel app-store subscriptions

Tassenger currently does not process in-app payments. If paid features are added in the future, deleting your account may not automatically cancel an app-store subscription or third-party billing arrangement. Follow the applicable Apple, Google, or payment-provider cancellation flow.
